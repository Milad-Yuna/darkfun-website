Jostoju · logotype · round 1

Two pen-drawn directions for جُسْتُجُو

Both are drawn from scratch with a digital reed pen. I draw the path of each stroke, and a cut nib is swept along it at the calligrapher's angle, so the thick and thin come from the pen, not from a font. The vowel marks are a second colour, as you asked.

Direction A

Keshideh کشیده

One long horizontal pull through the سـ is the exaggerated gesture. Everything else is compact around it. It reads well from far away and fits wide formats: the website header, the film end slate and signage.

Direction B

Pellekan پلکان

The stair. As in Nastaliq, each join steps down to the left, so the word falls from the ج at the top right to the و tail at the bottom left. It forms a compact block, close to the stacked lettering in your reference, and suits square formats: the avatar, posters and the title card of a film.

How it is built

The pen, the strokes, the vowels

The teal lines are the paths I drew. The pen is a cut nib, held at 68°, about one fifth of the word's height wide. Horizontal pulls come out heavy and rising strokes come out thin, the way a qalam behaves. The teeth of ت are written with the pen turned, so they stay strong.
