دستورهای تصویر جستجو

Art prompts

Eight images that turn Qajar paintings into new Jostoju artwork for the website. Every one hides the lost dot: one small madder-red diamond jewel that visitors can find on the site.

Model: Higgsfield Soul Cinema. Use one Qajar painting you love as the reference image; the prompt sets the new scene.
Settings: set the aspect ratio and 2k quality in the Higgsfield UI. They are not part of the prompt.
Blending: Soul Cinema takes one reference. To blend several paintings, use Nano Banana Pro, which accepts many references.
Save each result with the file name given and send it to me, or drop it into the repo. The site picks it up.

Wide cinematic film still inside a nineteenth-century Persian mirror hall at night. The walls and vaulted ceiling are covered in thousands of small diamond-shaped mirror tiles set in a faceted geometric mosaic, each facet catching warm candlelight in scattered gold sparks. On the left third of the frame a young Persian woman sits on a low embroidered bolster in a madder-red velvet jacket worked with gold thread, strings of pearls at her neck, black hair under a pearl and emerald diadem, kohl-lined almond eyes holding a calm, direct gaze into the lens. She raises a small round hand mirror; inside it glints a single madder-red diamond of light. Heavy red curtains are drawn aside behind her, brass lanterns glow on a Persian carpet. Warm low key light from the lanterns, soft 3:1 ratio, deep shadow in the corners. Composed with the frontal stillness of a Qajar court portrait. Palette of 60% varnished black and deep emerald, 30% madder red, 10% gold and pearl white. Rule of thirds. Photorealistic, ARRI Alexa LF, Cooke anamorphic lens, natural film grain.

A new nineteenth-century Persian court oil painting on canvas in the Qajar style. A young woman kneels against embroidered bolster cushions on a red patterned carpet, wearing a short madder-red jacket with gold embroidery, a full emerald-green skirt edged with lace, white stockings and pointed red slippers. She holds up a small lacquered hand mirror painted with roses; the mirror glows with pale white light like a tiny cinema screen. Almond eyes, joined black brows, a calm sideways glance at the viewer. A porcelain bowl of pomegranates and grapes rests beside her. On the carpet near her slipper lies one small loose madder-red diamond-shaped jewel, set apart from all the others. Flat decorative space, dark varnished background, gold leaf highlights, fine craquelure. Palette of 60% deep varnished brown-black, 30% madder red and emerald, 10% gold and pearl. Rule of thirds.

A wide nineteenth-century Persian court oil painting in the Qajar style, a long horizontal panorama. Thirty birds of many kinds, a hoopoe with its crest raised in front, nightingales, a peacock, a falcon and partridges, perch in one line along a gilded balustrade in a palace hall. They face an immense oval mirror in a carved gold frame on the left; in the mirror they appear as one great mythical bird with long flowing tail feathers in emerald, madder red and gold.
