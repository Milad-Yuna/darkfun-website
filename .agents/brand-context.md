# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: جستجو · Jostoju (pronounced *jos-to-JOO*; Persian for "the quest / the search")
- **Category**: AI film studio, making commissioned films and original stories
- **Description**: A bilingual (Persian + English) AI film studio that makes films with the visual language of the Persian carpet, Persian miniature painting and the naqqal's painted screen. Humans direct every frame.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch
- **Website**: Not live yet. Built in this repository (`darkfun-website`).

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Persian speakers first (Iran and the diaspora), then the world. (1) Brands and agencies, especially Persian-founded or Persian-market brands in Iran and diaspora hubs (Dubai, Toronto, Los Angeles, London, Istanbul, Hamburg), plus global brands that want a distinctive visual language. (2) Musicians and labels who need music videos. (3) Viewers of the studio's original films and series, especially second-generation diaspora and Persian-speaking audiences worldwide.
- **Key Problem**: Live-action production is slow and expensive and can't reach impossible worlds. Typical AI video looks generic, glossy and placeless, and it garbles Persian script. Representations of Iran swing between news-cycle politics and orientalist cliché.
- **Their Language**: "something that looks like home and like the future", "not another AI look", "Nowruz / Yalda campaign", "music video on an indie budget", "stories my grandmother told", "Persian that isn't broken"

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: The only AI film studio whose visual language is built from Persian image-making: the carpet's knot grid (naqsheh), the miniature's flat planes and frame-breaking compositions, and the naqqal's pardeh. Human-directed, natively bilingual, open about the tools it uses.
- **Competitors**: Promise (gen-AI studio, Hollywood/VC-backed), Asteria (AI studio built around licensed-data models), Secret Level (AI-assisted commercials for global brands), traditional production houses in Tehran, Dubai and Istanbul, and freelance AI creators or in-house teams using generation tools directly.
- **Market Position**: Premium niche. Culturally specific, director-led.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Curious, rooted, crafted, warm, quietly confident
- **Tone**: Warm and precise. More serious than playful, with dry Persian wit. Formal enough to be trusted, never stiff.
- **Archetype**: The Seeker, with a streak of the Magician. "A naqqal with a render farm."
- **Voice Admires**: Persian manuscript workshops (ketabkhaneh), A24's restraint, the craft language of master carpet weavers

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Talab / Seek first · Risheh / Rooted, not costumed · Dast / The hand in the machine · Rasti / Say how it was made · Heyrat / Wonder over spectacle
- **Vision**: To make Persian image-making a living language of world cinema again, built on craft, curiosity, honesty and wonder.
- **Mission**: We direct AI the way a master weaver directs a loom: with a design, a hand on every row and a reason for every knot. We make films in Persian and English, for clients and for ourselves, that people remember and that cultures recognise.

## Brand Architecture
- **Jostoju** (master brand)
- **Jostoju Kargah · کارگاه جستجو**: commissioned work (commercials, music videos, brand films)
- **Jostoju Pardeh · پردهٔ جستجو**: originals (shorts, series, features)
- **Jostoju Naqsheh · نقشهٔ جستجو**: future lab (workflows, education, tools)

## Messaging
- **Master tagline**: We make what cannot be found. · آنچه یافت می‌نشود، می‌سازیم.
- **Craft line**: Knot by knot, frame by frame. · گره به گره، قاب به قاب.
- **Origin line**: From pardeh to pixel. · از پرده تا پیکسل.

## Identity v2 · Qajar (summary; full spec in `brand/`)
- **Direction**: Deeply Persian and Qajar, bold, minimal and bright. Built on Qajar portraiture (dark varnished grounds, the frontal gaze, the drawn curtain, the arched niche, pearls, pomegranates), carpets and the words of Rumi and Attar.
- **Languages**: Persian first (RTL, the default), English second for the worldwide audience. Both are written natively.
- **Logotype**: «جُسْتُجُو» in Amiri Bold (SIL OFL), emboldened, with full vowel marks in the accent colour and diamond-knot dots. The long keshideh is the road. The dot of *ju* (seek) has left the last ج and waits ahead of the word (to the left), unattached, level with the road. A compact version without the long keshideh is used from 120 to 240 px.
- **Latin wordmark**: *jostoju* in Bodoni Moda ExtraBold, with knot dots on the j's.
- **Symbol**: The Missing Knot (گره گمشده). 24 diamond knots on a 5×5 lattice with the centre left open.
- **Colours**: Shab #16110E · Morvarid #F4EDE0 · Runas #A3261F · Zomorrod #1F5A46 · Tala #D4A640 · Lajvard #233C8E · Gol #E39AA6 · Kaghaz #E7D9BD
- **Type**: Amiri (FA display) · Vazirmatn (FA text) · Noto Nastaliq Urdu (verse) · Bodoni Moda (EN display) · Instrument Sans (EN text) · JetBrains Mono (credits)
- **Frames and pattern**: pearl strings, the arch, the curtain, the hashiyeh band, the toranj and gold dust.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal (12 months)**: Launch the studio with a bilingual website and showreel, win the first 6 to 10 commissioned projects, and release the first Pardeh original short.
- **Key Metrics**: Qualified inbound briefs per month, commission win rate, repeat clients, showreel and originals views and completion rate, followers on Instagram and YouTube, festival selections.
