Floor 5Who we are

We make horror the hard way.

No studio money. No giant sets. Just one room, one idea, and a scare you didn't see coming.

An elevator. A mirror. A road with no end. The places you know, turned against you.

We like it dark. We like it fun. Most of all, we like the second after the scream, when you laugh because you're still alive.

What we stand for

More nerve than money.

DarkFun is an independent horror studio. We make short, sharp horror on small budgets, and we make every frame look bigger than it cost.

  1. 01

    One room, one idea

    Limits are our method. A lift, a hallway, a car. The idea does the heavy lifting, not the budget.

  2. 02

    The everyday, turned

    We don't need castles. Our fear lives in places you use every day, so it follows you home.

  3. 03

    The grin

    A real scare and a dark laugh sit side by side. We're allowed to be funny. It's in our name.

  4. 04

    Filmmakers first

    Real lenses, real light, real sound. Ten years on festival screens. Small never means cheap.
