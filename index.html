About

Three people.
Thirty million views.

Dark Fun is an independent horror film studio run by a team of three. Directing, effects, editing, sound, distribution: it all happens in-house, by the same three people.

Our YouTube channel has passed 100,000 subscribers and 30 million views, with audiences across the United States, Europe and India. Most of them watch late. Most of them watch alone.
